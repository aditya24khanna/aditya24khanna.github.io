Install Moments

  1. Tap Share in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Moments from your home screen and capture there.

The installed app keeps its own storage. Moments saved here in Safari don't move across, so export a backup first, then import it in the app.
