Road of the day

MG Road

Tap the left or right half of the road to switch lanes; swipe up to speed up, down to slow down. On a keyboard: ← → to steer, ↑ ↓ for speed. Faster earns bonus metres; too slow and the bus behind starts honking. Use the horn (or H / Space) to make vehicles move, though nobody promises they'll move the right way. Three shock absorbers. Where the footpath is broken, people walk on the road. Filter coffee adds 50 m.
